# Getting help

- **Questions, setups, scene ideas:** [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/gasparoff/rays/discussions).
- **Something is broken:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/gasparoff/rays/issues/new?template=bug.yml) with your RAYS version, macOS version and gear.
- **Your light or controller isn't supported:** see [docs/hardware.md](docs/hardware.md), then open a
  [fixture request](https://github.com/gasparoff/rays/issues/new?template=new-fixture.yml) or
  [controller layout](https://github.com/gasparoff/rays/issues/new?template=new-controller.yml) issue.
- **Security issues:** never in public, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
